We are seeking an experienced Senior Data Engineer to join our growing data team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in building and maintaining scalable data pipelines and will be proficient in a variety of data technologies. You will be responsible for designing, implementing, and managing our data infrastructure to support our data analytics and business intelligence initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

- Data Pipeline Development: Design, build, and maintain robust and scalable ETL/ELT pipelines using tools like Informatica, Airflow, and Dataproc.

- Data Warehousing: Develop and manage our data warehouse on Google BigQuery, ensuring data is accurate, available, and easily accessible.

- Data Processing: Utilize Databricks and Python to process and transform large volumes of data efficiently.

- DevOps & Automation: Implement and manage CI/CD pipelines for data workflows using DevOps best practices and tools like Dataform and GitHub.

- Collaboration: Work closely with data scientists, analysts, and business stakeholders to understand their data needs and provide them with high-quality data.

- Data Modeling & BI (Optional): Develop data models and support BI initiatives by creating dashboards and reports in tools like Tableau.

- EPM Integration (Optional): Integrate data with our Anaplan platform to support connected financial and operational business planning.

Required Skills and Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.

- 3+ years of proven experience in a data engineering role.

- Strong proficiency in SQL and extensive experience with Google BigQuery.

- Hands-on experience with big data technologies such as Databricks and Dataproc.

- Experience with ETL/ELT tools like Informatica.

- Proficiency in orchestrating data pipelines with Airflow.

- Strong programming skills in Python.

- Solid understanding of DevOps principles and experience with Dataform and GitHub for CI/CD and version control.

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with BI visualization tools, particularly Tableau.

- Familiarity with EPM tools, especially Anaplan.

- Relevant certifications in Google Cloud Platform (GCP) or other data technologies.

- Excellent problem-solving skills and the ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment.
