About Northwind Health AI
Northwind Health AI builds clinical decision-support tools that help oncology researchers find the right cancer cell-line models faster, using large language models over multi-omics data.

The role
We are hiring an AI Engineer to build and evaluate LLM agents that sit on top of our research data platform. You will work end-to-end, from prototyping to production deployment on AWS, alongside scientists and product stakeholders.

What you'll do
- Design and build LLM agents with tool calling using LangChain or LangGraph
- Build retrieval (RAG) pipelines and guardrails that reduce hallucination
- Deploy services with Docker on AWS Lambda and maintain CI/CD with GitHub Actions
- Write evaluation suites (pytest, LLM evaluation) and monitor quality in production
- Explore and integrate heterogeneous datasets (exploratory data analysis, data quality checks)
- Communicate results to scientists and non-technical stakeholders

Requirements
- Strong Python and SQL
- Hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt engineering and agentic workflows
- Experience with AWS (Lambda, S3, IAM) and Docker
- Machine learning fundamentals with scikit-learn or PyTorch
- Experience building REST APIs with FastAPI
- 1+ years of experience in a data or ML role

Nice to have
- Bioinformatics or healthcare domain experience
- Kubernetes
- Fine-tuning (LoRA)
- Experience with MLflow or experiment tracking

We are a licensed sponsor and welcome applicants who need visa sponsorship. The role is hybrid in Bristol.
