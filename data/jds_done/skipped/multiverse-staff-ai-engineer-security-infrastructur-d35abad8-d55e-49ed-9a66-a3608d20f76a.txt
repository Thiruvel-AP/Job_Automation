THE AGENTIC WORK ENVIRONMENT TEAM

The Agentic Work Environment (AWE) owns the environment everyone at Multiverse works in, wherever they are in the world. That covers the devices, workplace tools and accounts colleagues use every day, the platform our product is built and run on, and the identity and access that decide who, or which agent, can do what across all of it. Product teams own what they build; we own the ground it stands on, the path it takes to production, and the baseline standards it meets on the way. Our mission is to make the safe way the fast way for every colleague and the agents they put to work.

For a security-minded AI engineer, that remit runs in two directions. In the product platform, you build security into the path itself: review agents that check every change against our baseline standards, one identity for customers, staff and agents, and detection and response through our SIEM and SOAR, so nobody, person or agent, has to remember to be secure. In the workplace, you secure the devices, tools and accounts colleagues around the world rely on. As more of them put agents to work, whatever their job title, you make sure every agent has its own identity, a named owner, scoped access and evals, so autonomy is earned on evidence rather than granted on trust.




WHAT YOUR DAY-TO-DAY WILL LOOK LIKE



Every day will be different, but you can expect it to include:

Build security into the path to production. You turn our baseline standards into checks that run on every change: policy as code, review agents that check each change against the baseline and a team's own standards, and gates matched to the risk of what's changing. Staying on the path should meet the security requirements without any extra work.

Secure the agents we and our colleagues build. You threat-model agent systems end to end, from prompt injection and tool misuse to data leaking through context and retrieval, and design the controls that stop it: scoped tool permissions, permission-aware retrieval, sandboxing, and human approval where the risk calls for it.

Give every agent an identity. You build the identity and access layer for agents. Each one has its own identity, a named owner, least-privilege access that is reviewed and can be revoked at once, and a full record of what it did.

Build detection and response with agents. You write detections and automated responses on our SIEM and SOAR, and build agents that triage alerts, gather context and carry out bounded, reversible responses, so people spend their time on the cases that need judgement.

Secure the workplace, worldwide. You apply the same approach to the devices, workplace tools and accounts colleagues use every day: automated controls rather than manual checklists, and access that is granted and removed as people join, move and leave.

Prove it holds with security evals. You build eval pipelines and red-team exercises that show whether our agents and controls stand up to attack, and produce compliance evidence as a by-product of the work rather than as screenshots for an auditor.

Influence technical direction. You have opinions about how things should be built, and you back them up with evidence. You contribute to architectural decisions, push back when the team is heading in the wrong direction, and propose better approaches.
