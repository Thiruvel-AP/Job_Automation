Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) 
At Capgemini Invent, we believe difference drives change. As inventive transformation consultants, we blend our strategic, creative and scientific capabilities, collaborating closely with clients to deliver cutting-edge solutions. This role sits within our Applied AI Practice, at the sharp end of that mission: embedded directly with client teams, turning ambiguous problems into shipped, production-grade AI systems. 
We're looking for Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), senior technologists who combine deep hands-on engineering with the judgement and presence to operate as a trusted advisor at client sites. This isn't a traditional consulting or traditional engineering role. It's both, at once. You'll be the person a client CTO calls when something needs to be architected, built, and defended in the same week. 
In this role, you will: 

- Embed directly within client environments (often on-site or hybrid) to design and build bespoke AI and agentic solutions against live, high-stakes business problems.
- Own technical architecture end to end: from initial discovery and stakeholder workshops through to production deployment, taking personal accountability for delivery quality.
- Move fluidly between hands-on coding (backend, frontend, infrastructure) and senior stakeholder engagement, including presenting architecture, trade-offs, and roadmap directly to Director and C-suite audiences.
- Rapidly prototype solutions in ambiguous, fast-moving environments, then harden them into robust, scalable, secure production systems.
- Build and run evaluation frameworks that give clients confidence in model and system behaviour, treating evals as a first-class part of the delivery lifecycle rather than an afterthought.
- Act as a technical anchor for delivery teams, setting engineering standards, unblocking hard problems, and mentoring engineers around evaluation-driven development and agentic best practice.
- Shape commercial conversations: sizing effort, identifying scope risk, and contributing to how engagements are structured and priced.
- Build and protect trust with client technical leadership, acting as the credible link between what's technically possible and what the business actually needs.

Your Profile 
Seniority and Experience 

- Significant experience as a senior or principal-level software engineer or architect, with a track record of owning systems end to end in production.
- Demonstrated experience operating client-facing or customer-facing in a consulting, professional services, or startup environment, comfortable being judged on both code and client outcomes.
- Experience working directly with senior stakeholders (CTO, VP Engineering, Director level), including running workshops and presenting technical strategy to non-technical audiences. 
Technical Expertise 

- Backend Development: Deep experience building production microservices, ideally Python or TypeScript.
- Frontend Development: Strong experience with React and TypeScript, using frameworks such as Next.js or Vite.
- AI and Agentic Systems: Hands-on experience designing and deploying agentic architectures using frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, or the Anthropic Agent SDK, including tool use, MCP servers, and multi-agent orchestration.
- LLM Experience: Practical, production experience with LLM providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Llama) and an informed view on model selection trade-offs.
- Cloud Platforms: Strong proficiency in AWS or Azure or GCP, including designing for scale, security, and cost.
- Containerisation and Orchestration: Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and Docker Compose.
- Infrastructure as Code: Proficiency with Terraform or equivalent.
- Evaluation and Monitoring: Experience with evaluation-driven development and LLM observability tooling such as Langsmith, Langfuse, Braintrust, or similar.
- CI/CD: Comfortable owning pipelines end to end (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins).
- Database Management: Experience with vector databases (ChromaDB, Pinecone, PGVector, Qdrant, MongoDB Vector Search); familiarity with NoSQL (MongoDB preferred) and SQL (PostgreSQL) databases. 
Commercial and Stakeholder Skills 

- Commercial awareness: able to reason about effort, scope, and value trade-offs, and contribute to how work is priced and structured.
- Excellent written and verbal communication, able to adapt technical depth for engineering, delivery, and executive audiences.
- Comfortable holding a room: presenting architecture decisions and defending technical positions to senior, sometimes skeptical, stakeholders. 
Work Methodology 
Experience working in agile teams, with the maturity to operate independently when embedded on client sites.
