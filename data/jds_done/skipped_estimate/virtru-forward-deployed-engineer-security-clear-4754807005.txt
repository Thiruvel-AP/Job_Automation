About Virtru:

While the rest of the security industry obsesses over locking data down to prevent it from being lost or stolen, we're doing something fundamentally different at Virtru. We're setting data free so that you can intentionally share it with others, but without sacrificing security, privacy, or control.

We've created both a suite of powerful data protection applications and an open platform that's sparking an ecosystem of innovation. Through the Trusted Data Format (TDF) open standard, we're not just protecting data; we're creating a new paradigm where security enables sharing rather than preventing it.

Think of us as the Android of data protection: a robust platform with an open core that developers and partners can build upon, coupled with our own best-in-class applications that showcase what's possible when you reimagine security from the ground up.

Backed by Iconiq Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners, Foundry Capital, and Tiger Global, we're helping Fortune 500 companies and government agencies discover that true data security means having the freedom to share, collaborate, and innovate — without compromise.
Compensation: £125,000-160,000/year

Team & Position Details:

Virtru protects data wherever it travels, for customers ranging from commercial enterprises to the Department of Defense, the Intelligence Community, and government and defense organizations in allied nations, and we are growing with government and defense customers in the United Kingdom and Europe. Many of these customers run Virtru inside their own classified networks, where the work happens on site next to their engineers.

As a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer in the Delivery Engineering organization, you'd be helping to build a founder team focused on making these deployments simple, easy, and fast, and you'd be our first forward deployed engineer based in the UK. You'll be the engineer who can change the software in the room for UK customers: building the integrations, policy enforcement points (the components that enforce access policy inside a customer's application), and deployment tooling they need and cannot get from the product alone, and owning the technical outcome of each deployment from zero to one and on into production.

You will work with the UK account team and our systems integrator partners, with solutions architects who shape each engagement, with forward deployed and deployment engineers in the US, with QA to prove what you build before it ships, and with our product engineering and Platform Engineering counterparts, who need what you learn in the field written down in a form they can act on.

The impact of this role is company and product wide, heavily enabling our goals to scale Virtru, partner, and customer led deployments non-linearly without having to throw more humans at the problem. You'll be expected to come up with AI-native design patterns that will increase our velocity without compromising our high bar for quality and security. The right candidate is excited about working on a small, nimble, highly autonomous and empowered team where everyone is expected to make high judgement, high velocity decisions independently and with urgency. If you want a role where the connection between what you build and what the customer achieves requires no explaining, this is one.

As a Senior Forward Deployed Engineer, your responsibilities will include:

- Build and ship software inside UK customer environments. Integrations, policy enforcement points, connectors, and deployment tooling, written in Go, Python, Java, or Rust, reviewed, tested with QA, and delivered into environments Virtru does not operate.

- Own the technical outcome of each deployment. From the first environment review through production and upgrade, you are the engineer accountable for the platform working in the customer's systems, across Kubernetes, Windows estates, identity providers, and air-gapped classified networks.

- Turn field lessons into product decisions. Write architecture decision records that weigh a product fix against a field workaround, bring them to our product engineering teams with evidence, and follow them through to a decision.

- Diagnose failures down to source code, whether the cause sits in our software, in the customer's environment, or in the space between them.

- Work with systems integrator partners so they can deliver standard deployments, and hand them what you build with documentation, test evidence, and a clear way to report back.

- Support the UK account team and solutions architects in early technical conversations, so what customers are offered is something we can deploy.

- Package what you build so it installs, upgrades, and runs in disconnected environments with no path to the internet.

- Work inside codebases other teams own, and leave them better tested and documented than you found them.

- Explain your designs to customer security reviewers and accreditors, and answer their follow-up questions.

- Use AI coding agents as part of your daily workflow, and help the team learn which patterns raise quality and speed together.

- This role requires on-site work at customer locations in the UK at least 50% of the time, and may require travel to customer and partner sites in the UK, Europe, and the United States.

Skills that will help you thrive in this role:

- Must haves

- An active UK security clearance at: SC at a minimum with Developed Vetting (DV) or higher preferred. This role will work in classified environments at least 50% of the time.

- You ship production code today. You write and review code in Go, Python, Java, or Rust as part of your current job, you have owned a change to a codebase someone else wrote from design through release, and you can walk us through it.

- You can make software work in an environment you did not build. You have diagnosed identity, certificate, or Kubernetes failures in a customer or production environment from logs, without the original author on the call.

- Nice to haves

- Kubernetes across more than one distribution, such as OpenShift, Rancher, or RKE2, including Helm packaging.

- Delivering software into air-gapped environments: offline registries, bundle transfer, and upgrades with no network.

- Identity and authorization integration: OIDC, SAML, Entra ID, PingFederate, and attribute-based access control (ABAC).

- Windows and .NET depth: Windows Services, signed installers, Active Directory, and Group Policy.

- Working knowledge of the UK Government Security Classifications Policy and NCSC guidance from the delivery side.

- Experience in NATO or coalition environments where data is shared across nations.

- Familiarity with Zero Trust security architecture and concepts such as Policy Enforcement and Decision Points, cryptography, and identity.

- Experience building with AI coding agents.

- Prior forward deployed, embedded, or customer-site engineering work with UK government or defense customers.

- Where this role may not be a fit

- Having stepped away from writing code. If your recent work is advising, configuring, or reviewing rather than writing and shipping code yourself, this role may not be for you.

- Needing a large local team around you. You'll be the first forward deployed engineer in the UK, working with partners and with colleagues in the US across time zones. If you need a team in the same office, this role may not be for you.

- Inability to earn trust and credibility with partners and customers. You may be the person who has to explain to a skeptical government security team why our software is safe, or to an internal VP of Engineering why we need to prioritize critical platform features. Those have to be conversations you are willing and excited to have.
