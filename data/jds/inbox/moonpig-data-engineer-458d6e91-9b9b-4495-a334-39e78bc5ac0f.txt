Key Responsibilities
- Design, build and maintain data pipelines to ingest, transform and load data from a variety of sources into our data warehouse.

- Optimise, maintain and troubleshoot pipelines so consistent, high-quality data is up to date and available.

- Develop and maintain data models that support business intelligence and analytics needs.

- Ensure the quality, performance and security of our data infrastructure.

- Follow best practices for security and infrastructure.

- Create and maintain relevant documentation.

- Collaborate with other engineering teams to build and deploy data-driven products and features.

About You
- 5+ years of experience in data engineering.

- Strong experience with SQL and ETL/ELT tools such as Fivetran and dbt.

- Experience with data warehousing on Snowflake.

- Confidence interacting with databases and web APIs and processing data.

- Good understanding of relational databases and data modelling best practices.

- Awareness of data governance and security.

- Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills.

- Strong communication and collaboration skills.

- Technical experience with Snowflake, Fivetran, dbt, Git source control, MS DevOps, GitHub Actions and Python.

- Azure experience is a plus.

- Experience with orchestration tooling such as Airflow is nice to have.

- Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform is nice to have.

Our Tech Environment
- Snowflake, Fivetran, dbt, Git source control, MS DevOps, GitHub Actions and Python.

- Data warehouses, data pipelines, databases, web APIs and cloud computing platforms.

- Azure, Airflow and Terraform are relevant additional technologies for this role.

How We Get There
- We use applied engineering best practices to solve real-world problems and make a positive impact on the business.

- We collaborate with teams across the organisation to understand data requirements and make data available through secure, stable and GDPR-compliant single sources of truth.

- We enable reporting, analytics and ad-hoc queries while focusing on data quality, performance and security.
