Job Description

Overview

Join a genuinely collaborative team whose diverse expertise delivers innovative solutions to complex engineering challenges worldwide. As an experienced data scientist, you will work across the data lifecycle in a fast-paced Agile environment, helping keep people, critical businesses and communication networks connected and secure. The role includes some client-site work, with flexible and remote working options, career development, training and a competitive reward package.
The successful candidate will develop insightful, machine learning and data-driven solutions that support defensive cyber operations, mission data exploitation and operational decision-making within a secure national security environment.
Working within Agile, multidisciplinary product teams, you will collaborate with Product Owners, Business Analysts, Data Engineers, Data Visualisers and cyber specialists to transform large-scale data into trusted, operationally useful data products. The role requires a strong foundation in data science together with practical experience operating within modern data platforms, MLOps environments and software engineering workflows.

Your role

Data Science & Analytics

- Design, develop and deploy statistical, insightful, AI and machine learning solutions to solve operational challenges.
- Explore large and complex datasets to identify trends, patterns and actionable insights.
- Apply appropriate machine learning, predictive analytics and data science techniques to real-world problems.
- Develop and evaluate insightful models using robust performance and validation methodologies.
- Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Contribute to development of innovative AI, machine learning and advanced analytics capabilities.
Data Product Delivery

- Ensure large datasets are managed appropriately across staging, modelling, analytics and presentation layers.
- Contribute to the design and delivery of high-quality data products that support Cyber Capability requirements.
- Work collaboratively with Product Owners and Business Analysts to translate user needs into insightful solutions.
- Support data quality assurance, governance and reproducibility across insightful workflows.
Platform & Engineering

- Develop and maintain production-quality Python and SQL code.
- Use Git-based source control and branch management practices across multiple deployment environments.
- Work with Spark and PySpark to process and analyse large-scale datasets.
- Develop and manage machine learning workflows within an MLOps environment.
- Use tooling such as MLflow, VS Code or JupyterHub for model development, experimentation and deployment.
- Work with technologies to access, manage and analyse data at scale (e.g., Apache Iceberg and Trino).
Delivery Excellence

- Participate fully in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, backlog refinement, demonstrations and retrospectives.
- Produce high-quality technical documentation and knowledge-sharing materials.
- Contribute to continuous improvement of data science and MLOps practices.

About you

Essential Skills & Experience

- Candidates should typically bring at least three years of relevant professional experience, although applicants with less experience and strong evidence of capability will also be considered.
- Strong Python and SQL skills, with experience developing production-quality insightful, statistical, AI and machine-learning solutions.
- Experience working with large, complex, structured and unstructured datasets, including data preparation, exploratory analysis, feature engineering and model validation.
- Practical experience with Git, Agile delivery and collaborative software-development workflows.
- Experience using distributed data-processing and MLOps tools such as Spark, PySpark, MLflow, JupyterHub or equivalent technologies.
- Understanding of data-product lifecycles, data quality, governance, reproducibility and auditable insightful workflows.
- Ability to translate operational or business needs into insightful solutions and communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Effective collaboration within multidisciplinary teams, supported by strong problem-solving, documentation and critical-thinking skills.
Desirable Skills & Experience

- Advanced statistical modelling, experimental design, forecasting, anomaly detection, model optimisation or explainability.
- Deployment and operation of machine-learning models, including monitoring, drift detection and performance management.
- Awareness of modern lakehouse or enterprise data platforms and technologies such as Trino and Apache Iceberg.
- Experience in NLP, large language models, Generative AI, deep learning, graph analytics.
- Responsible AI or retrieval-augmented generation.
- Delivery of data products or decision-support capabilities in secure, governed or operational environments.
- Stakeholder engagement, workshop facilitation and presentation of insightful findings to senior audiences.
Personal Attributes

- Curious, insightful and outcome-focused, with a commitment to technical excellence and continuous learning.
- Collaborative and comfortable working through complexity and ambiguity.
- Able to balance innovation with operational reliability, governance and security.
- Motivated to deliver data products that create measurable user and mission impact.
Qualifications

- A degree in data science, computer science, software engineering, mathematics, statistics, physics, engineering or another quantitative discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
- Evidence of relevant continuing professional development.
- A postgraduate qualification or recognised professional certification in data science, AI, machine learning, data engineering, cloud platforms, Agile delivery, Responsible AI or security is advantageous but not essential.
