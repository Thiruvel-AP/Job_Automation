FRP Advisory are a UK-wide professional services organisation investing in Microsoft Fabric, Power BI and modern data engineering to provide reliable, well-governed data for reporting, analytics and AI. 
Role Overview 
This hands-on role sits within the Applications and Data team, working alongside an existing Data Engineer to develop and support our Microsoft Fabric-based enterprise data platform. 
This role will report day to day to the Data & Analytics Lead, who will set priorities, provide technical direction and coordinate Data Engineering and Power BI delivery. Together, you will build reliable pipelines, dimensional models and transformations that make trusted data available across the business. 
Key Responsibilities 

- Lead and develop the data team, including Data Engineering, with clear priorities and responsibilities
- Guide technical work across reporting, data modelling, integration and engineering
- Design and maintain Power BI reports, dashboards, semantic models and supporting data models
- Set Power BI standards for architecture, development, governance, deployment and reuse
- Work with stakeholders to understand requirements, agree priorities and manage delivery
- Work with Data Engineering to build reliable data pipelines and connect source systems, Microsoft Fabric and Power BI
- Maintain standards for data quality, security, performance, documentation and support
- Train selected technical colleagues to contribute to Power BI work alongside their main roles
- Track the Microsoft Fabric roadmap and recommend relevant features for FRP
- Travel to regional offices when needed for meetings, delivery and training Qualifications 

- Strong commercial experience of Kimball dimensional modelling and dbt transformations.
- Experience using Git and Azure DevOps for source control, testing and CI/CD.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with complex data structures.
- Experience building and supporting enterprise data warehouses or similar analytical platforms.
- Experience developing reliable ETL or ELT processes in the Medallion architecture.
- Experience with Microsoft Fabric data engineering, including Pyspark and dbt.
- Practical knowledge of data quality, testing, monitoring and operational support.
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
- Clear communication and the ability to work independently while collaborating with the Data & Analytics Lead, technical colleagues and business stakeholders. 
Experience in the following areas would also be useful: 

- Materialised Lake Views
- Apache Spark or Python.
- Power BI semantic modelling.
- APIs and cloud business system integration.
- Data governance, lineage and quality frameworks.
- Professional services experience.
