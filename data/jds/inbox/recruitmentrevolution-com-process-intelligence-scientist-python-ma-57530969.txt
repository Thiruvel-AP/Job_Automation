The Keit Story 
At Keit, we're passionate about making complex industrial processes easier to understand, monitor and improve. 
Our IRmadillo spectrometer platform provides advanced, real-time chemical analysis, while our IRis software platform combines spectroscopy, laboratory measurements and operational data to deliver meaningful process insights. 
Today, our technology helps customers in the fuel ethanol industry monitor and optimise fermentation. 
But we're only scratching the surface. 
Your Role in the Story 
As our Process Intelligence Scientist, you'll work at the intersection of machine learning, chemistry, industrial processes and software development. 
You'll develop predictive models, improve existing chemometric capabilities and explore new ways to turn complex process data into practical recommendations for customers. 
Working closely with chemometricians, software developers and customer engineering teams, you'll understand real operational challenges and develop solutions that create measurable value. 
This is a hands-on opportunity to combine technical problem-solving, experimentation and customer collaboration while helping shape the future of our IRis platform.
