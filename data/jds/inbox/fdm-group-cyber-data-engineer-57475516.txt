About The Role 
FDM is a global business and technology consultancy seeking a Cyber Data Engineer to work for our client within the Financial Services sector. This is initially a 6-12 month contract with the potential to extend and will be a hybrid role that will be based in Glasgow. 

Our client is seeking a Cyber Data Engineer to join its globally distributed security analytics team. 

The team is responsible for developing and supporting platforms used by cyber functions including security response, investigations and insider-threat teams. A key focus of the position will be supporting the migration of the firm’s cyber data lake from Splunk to CyberELK, an Elasticsearch-based security analytics platform. 

This role would suit an engineer with a strong grounding in Python, infrastructure automation, Linux and data pipelines who is interested in developing deeper expertise in Elasticsearch and cybersecurity engineering. 

Responsibilities 

A successful applicant will contribute to several important initiatives including: 

- Developing automation tools that integrate with in-house developed configuration management frameworks and APIs, including our AI-assisted onboarding tooling
- Onboarding data sources into CyberELK with appropriate field extractions and Elastic Common Schema (ECS) mapping
- Tuning and optimising Elasticsearch cluster performance, including sharding, index lifecycle management (ILM) and ingest pipelines
- Providing consultancy to internal clients and stakeholders
- Working as an escalation point to perform complex troubleshooting, working with other infrastructure teams to resolve issues About You 

- Infrastructure automation and integration experience, ideally using Python and Ansible
- A solid understanding of Operating Systems and Networking concepts: Linux/Unix system administration, HTTP and encryption.
- Interest in using AI-assisted engineering tools (e.g. GitHub Copilot, Claude Code) to accelerate delivery
- Good understanding of software version control, deployment & build tools using DevOps SDLC practices (Git, Jenkins, Jira)
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills
- Excellent verbal & written communication skills
- Appreciation of Agile methodologies, specifically Kanban 
Desired Skills 

- Prior experience deploying and managing large-scale Elasticsearch platforms in production; Splunk experience is valuable given our ongoing migration
- Experience with Elastic Stack ingest and pipeline tooling (e.g. Logstash, Beats or equivalent)

- Experience with data pipelines on a major Cloud Service Provider like GCP, Azure or AWS Data engineering and configuration experience including writing and testing field extractions/parsers using regular expressions
