Data Analyst WFM – The role 

- Analyse workforce deployment, labour cost and productivity data to provide actionable business insights and support operational decision-making. 
- Collaborate with product leadership, workforce management teams and operational stakeholders to understand business questions and identify reporting requirements. 
- Develop and enhance dashboards, reports and analytical outputs using SQL and BI tools within the agreed workforce management product model. 
- Apply consistent labour metrics and calculation definitions to ensure reporting accuracy and business confidence. 
- Investigate anomalies, validate calculations and reconcile unexpected results with data owners and stakeholders. 
- Identify opportunities to improve reporting efficiency, reduce manual processes and increase the reuse of trusted analytics. 
- Support labour intelligence initiatives used by client-facing, operational and financial teams across the business. 
- Apply enterprise data standards, privacy controls and responsible data practices when handling workforce information. 
- Communicate findings, reporting changes and business insights clearly to non-technical stakeholders. 
What we're looking for 

- Experience using SQL and business intelligence tools to analyse data and create meaningful reporting solutions. 
- Understanding of workforce deployment, labour cost and productivity analytics. 
- Experience working with semantic models, validating calculations and producing reliable management information. 
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to investigate trends, anomalies and data quality issues. 
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain findings and recommendations clearly to a range of stakeholders. 
- Strong collaboration skills and the ability to build effective relationships across operational and central teams. 
- Sound judgement when handling sensitive workforce data and applying appropriate privacy and access controls. 
- Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment while managing multiple priorities and delivering high-quality insights
