About the role
To deliver on this mission, we are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join the Tech Flex team.
The Tech Flex team was established in early 2026, and has been growing strongly since then. The team is focused on greenfield opportunities at the cutting edge of Octopus' flexibility strategy, and aims to enable rapid response to opportunities, globally and across a wide range of domains. 
Time series forecasting is a critical capability across essentially all of our workstreams. Through this role, we aim to build a new capability within an already data-literate team. The intent is that this role will:
Act as an owner of time series forecasting expertise, and so will work across any project that has a forecasting component
Will not be explicitly limited to time series forecasting. Our team’s growth will continue to be dynamic, and so we expect this role to engage with backend engineering, data engineering, MLOps, machine learning, essentially to act as a ‘full stack data scientist’ with a specialisation on time series forecasting
We are building the next generation of products and tools to make energy more flexible. These include our flagship Intelligent Octopus product automating demand shift for 300,000 customers with electric cars, home batteries or smart heating devices, saving 2 million customers money through Saving Sessions and Free Electricity Sessions, and managing 568MW of large grid-scale batteries to balance the system. The team develops products that are used by millions of customers, helping boost renewables use and decarbonise energy systems worldwide.

What you'll do
- 

Acting as the Tech Flex’s first full-time data scientist, establish the team’s DS capability, lead the team’s DS technical direction and represent the team to the cross-team DS community

- 

Approach projects with a ‘product-oriented’ mindset, to discover requirements and technical challenges that can only be seen with a DS lens, then to communicate risks, tradeoffs and opportunities to team leads 

- 

Establish greenfield forecasting systems upon the foundational layer of data engineering and warehousing that has been established in our own AWS environment(s)

- 

Work closely with the centralised market forecasting team, extending their system to meet the needs of our platform

- 

Interface with the established data warehouse, built on Databricks

- 

Upskill data-literate backend engineers, act as the team's forecasting expert, and as a multiplier on other engineers' impact
