4. Cover Letter  [READY; letter]
Your team must deliver actionable insights and build data-driven solutions. That is the kind of problem I have been working on. I check the data before I trust a model: I audited 859,494 training examples and found 44,369 duplicates, and for my AstraZeneca-affiliated MSc dissertation I combined 14 research datasets so the results rested on trusted data. When my gene models scored 0.807 on training data but only 0.545 on new data, I reported it plainly instead of hiding it.

I placed 3rd at Lloyd's Banking Group's datathon by spotting rare suspicious transactions, about 1 in 200, and in my MSc I used proper statistics, planning the sample size first and then running ANOVA tests, to pick the best of 6 algorithm settings. At 180 Degrees Consulting I helped turn a startup's vague idea into a working tool that is now in live, day-to-day use; the client's founder called it "stable, production-ready". I also built a model that predicts which bank customers are likely to leave, and I have presented results live to client stakeholders who were not technical.

Your ad asks for proficiency with Python and standard data science libraries, and that is the part of this role I most want to grow into.

Could we spend 20 minutes on what the first problem in this role would be?
