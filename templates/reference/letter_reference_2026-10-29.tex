\noindent
Hi Graphcore team,

Graphcore builds AI systems from silicon to software, where models must fit real memory limits; my Llama 3.1 8B fine-tune had to fit one Colab T4, and a 4-bit QLoRA adapter on 0.52\% of the parameters lifted exact-answer accuracy from 16.2\% to 27.3\% against a pre-registered +5\,pp criterion, scored on a held-out golden test set.

Then I traced the result and could explain it: accuracy among finished answers was 50.0\% vs 51.9\%, so the gain came from more answers finishing inside the 384-token cap, not stronger reasoning. I reported it that way.

In MSc Data Science deep-learning labs I built PyTorch neural networks (MLP, CNN on CIFAR-10, ResNet50 transfer learning, LSTM, GRU) with Adam optimisers. On a GPU project I made about 282 minutes of T4 training resumable and cut RAM from 8.2 to 5.5 GB.

At AstraZeneca I owned a Python LLM agent from raw data to AWS Lambda, tested by a three-tier, $\sim$65-test evaluation; at 180 Degrees Consulting I led the architecture for a five-person team and explained the prototype in a live demo.

The Graduate Programme appeals because it puts machine learning experiments next to the people who build the chips and systems they run on.

One honest gap: I have trained on one GPU only, never multi-GPU or distributed, so scaling is where I will learn.
